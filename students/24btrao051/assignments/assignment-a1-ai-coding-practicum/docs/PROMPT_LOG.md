## Why these techniques where they were

- **Zero-shot** for scaffold + tests: high-frequency patterns the model knows cold; over-specifying would just add noise.
- **Few-shot** for error-envelope + docstrings: the *shape* of the output was the requirement; 2–5 examples pin it far harder than adjectives.
- **Chain-of-thought** for netting + rounding bug: both involve arithmetic/representation subtleties (float midpoints, transfer minimality) where watching the steps distinguishes a correct fix from a lucky one.
