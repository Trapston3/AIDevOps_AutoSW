Contract:
    def compute_settlement(balances: dict[str, float]) -> list[Settlement]
where Settlement is a small dataclass with fields: from_ (str), to (str), amount (float).

Semantics:
- Input maps member name -> signed balance. Positive = the group owes them (they overpaid). Negative = they owe the group.
- Balances always sum to ~0 (floating point tolerance).
- Output must be the MINIMAL list of transfers that clears all balances: debtors pay creditors directly, never route money through intermediaries.
- Round every transfer amount to 2 decimal places.
- Greedy largest-debtor-to-largest-creditor matching is acceptable for v1.

Worked example to reason through first:
    balances = {"A": -12.0, "B": -6.0, "C": 18.0}
    -> [Settlement(from_="A", to="C", amount=12.0), Settlement(from_="B", to="C", amount=6.0)]
